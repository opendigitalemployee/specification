# Personal-agent self-description procedure

**Experimental application procedure · 0.2.0 · 4 October 2026.** A bounded first slice of [issue #4](https://github.com/opendigitalemployee/specification/issues/4), using SELF-01 from the personal foundation proposal in [issue #2](https://github.com/opendigitalemployee/specification/issues/2). These issue proposals are not assigned published profile versions. Output schemas have their own versions.

## Useful result

A reader can identify whom the agent serves, its assignment, instructions and memory, selected methods, authority, dependencies, and questions requiring source analysis or discussion. Separate stable definition, current assignment, observations, person-specific information and method changes.

Start from the [blank collection](personal-description-template/0.1.0/README.md) and compare the [fictional Aster example](../../../examples/personal-description/0.2.0/README.md). Both use the [experimental collection contract](../../../docs/agents/self-description-contract.md). No private real-agent profile is distributed.

## Procedure

1. **Establish the assignment.** State beneficiary, present use, useful result and responsible reviewer. Keep the relationship and assignment distinct. Record unknown authority or outcomes.
2. **Select authorized sources.** Inspect current instructions, identity or role files, relevant decisions, selected memory, methods and environment facts. Preserve dates and source revisions. Do not load a deep personal dossier merely to fill a form. A source may describe a rule without proving behavior.
3. **Write reflective claims.** Record basis, source references, scope, date and review state. Distinguish source-backed facts, observations, self-reports and inferences. Preserve conflicts. A one-off request does not establish a permanent trait or standing authority.
4. **Describe composition.** Record native files and entry order, selected shared basis, memory, methods, and data/runtime requirements. Give dependencies a purpose, relationship, locator and known or unknown revision. Distinguish available, selected, used and evidenced skills. See the [composition guide](../../../docs/agents/component-description.md).
5. **Describe authority and continuity.** Record allowed, forbidden and unresolved actions with their basis. Distinguish tools from permission and instructions from enforcement. Name included files and external or missing resources. Retain unknown runtime versions and budgets. Recheck freshness, access and authority before continuing from an old description.
6. **Assess the description.** Fill all [25 design and 15 quality records](../../../docs/agents/self-diagnosis.md). Reference actual claims, explain each status and route partial/missing answers. Keep agreement and behavior evidence independent. Save the assessment as part of the collection, pinning the exact profile version and digest.
7. **Finish and check.** Pin description artifacts and deliberately included native files in the index. Run the structural check. Review the result with the responsible person without claiming agreement in advance. Keep previous versions.

## Author completion

Finish when the declared use is clear; known claims have their basis and limits; selected composition and meaningful dependencies are recorded; every checklist item is assessed; unresolved questions have a next route; and references/digests are consistent. Substantive accuracy still requires review after the automated check passes.

Partial and missing answers are valid outcomes. Empty template lists mean not yet supplied. An undefined Work is different from a fabricated contract. Tags for conditions or areas do not count as answers. Owner agreement, behavior and readiness are not inferred from author completion.

Output ends at these files and explicit dependencies. Traversal, dependency closure, tail cutting, summaries, archives and restoration trials are subsequent scoped activities. See the [description and backup boundary](../../../docs/agents/description-and-restoration.md#description-and-backup-have-separate-deliverables).

## Disclosure and updates

Keep the primary collection in owner-controlled storage. For disclosure, select authorized facts/files, give a rewritten projection its own identity/version and provenance, and retain omissions and limits. A derivative cannot inherit its source identity or hash. Validation does not authorize disclosure.

Revisions to person-specific context, general methods and authority are independent. Assess each new profile version separately. Old records must not reinstate revoked access or make stale observations current.

## Fit with the standard and remaining work

Use Role/Work, Skill/ActivationBinding, Source/Storage, Policy, InteractionContract and native resources where their existing semantics fit. The [three Check definitions](personal-self-description.checks.json) use existing kinds; they are definitions, not trials. The description is not a Run, DEP manifest or normative ClientAgent. Organization-only personal ownership remains unresolved; no fictional organization is required.

The slice provides a reusable file contract and an authored synthetic example. Independent author repetition remains open. The original issue additionally requests connected multi-day sources, completed onboarding and useful work, feedback followed by approved initiative, eight situations, separate training/evaluation and improvement/regression results. Those remain later increments with their own acceptance; this release does not close the issue.
